# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring managers, engineering leads, collaborators, and clients evaluating Inam Ul Haq's DevOps, cloud infrastructure, automation, and software engineering work.

## Product Purpose

Present Inam's technical identity, selected systems, operating experience, and contact paths through a portfolio that demonstrates production-minded engineering rather than merely listing skills.

## Positioning

The portfolio makes infrastructure work visible through an interactive terminal, architecture-led project proof, and a scroll-controlled 3D DevOps lifecycle.

## Operating Context

Visitors scan the opening identity, inspect project architecture and tooling, review professional experience and education, download the CV, and continue to GitHub, LinkedIn, email, or phone.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Framer Motion, and Three.js are the established implementation stack.
- The terminal and 3D lifecycle are reusable portfolio artifacts and must remain scroll-driven.
- Motion must remain understandable with keyboard navigation and reduced-motion preferences.
- Project claims, employers, education, links, and contact details must come from repository evidence.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Inam Ul Haq.
- Professional focus: DevOps engineering, cloud infrastructure, automation, and production systems.
- The supplied dark portfolio reference is binding inspiration for atmosphere, tactile grain, restrained navigation, and strong identity typography. It is not a layout to copy literally.
- The portfolio should feel technically credible, cinematic, and authored without becoming a generic neon dashboard.

## Evidence on Hand

- CV: `public/InamUlHaq_CV.pdf`.
- Project image: `public/mistle.jpg`.
- Project and architecture content: `src_legacy/components/ProjectsSection.tsx` and `src_legacy/components/diagrams`.
- Experience and education: `src_legacy/components/ExperienceSection.tsx` and `src_legacy/components/EducationSection.tsx`.
- Contact and social links: `src_legacy/components/ContactInfo.tsx`.
- Existing interactive proof: `src/components/ScrollBeamHero.tsx` and `src/components/artifacts/devops-infinity`.
- No testimonials, customer logos, or independently verified performance benchmarks are on hand; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Show systems and decisions before skill inventories.
2. Use motion to explain sequence, state, and lifecycle.
3. Keep every professional claim traceable to existing evidence.
4. Make ambitious visual work remain fast, legible, and controllable.
5. Give every section a clear route to deeper proof or contact.
